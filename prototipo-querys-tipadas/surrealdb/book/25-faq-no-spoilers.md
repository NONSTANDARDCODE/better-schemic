### Where did the idea for the story come from?

I originally wanted to write a textbook that followed the story of the Enchanted Knights, which was so beautifully illustrated in [this book](https://archive.org/details/gri_33125012874737/page/n24/mode/1up) in the early 1900s. But it isn't long enough to make for a full textbook's worth of content, and the story itself isn't that well known either.

I then began thinking about a library of books instead of a single book, and whether the book could be based on the construction of a library using SurrealDB. That felt a bit closer, but even that would just end up being a single project that would be tough to stretch over a full textbook.

At that point my imagination kicked in, and I began thinking about what sort of library it could be...then all of a sudden the story began to take form and I knew I had found it.

### Why the word renaissance in the title?

Renaissance means rebirth, and there is quite a bit of that in the book.

- The story itself involves bringing back the world of the past (that is, the world of the late 21st century), similar to the Renaissance in Europe which involved rediscovering a lot of the knowledge that had been lost.

- It's a surreal (lowercase) Renaissance because the knowledge in this case was straight up given to the main character.

- It's also a Surreal (uppercase) Renaissance, because SurrealDB is finally being used again after centuries.
